DineEase: Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This page explains how to ask for your DineEase account, or the personal data you gave to the DineEase app, to be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to minahilamir2012@gmail.com with the subject DineEase account deletion request.
  2. Restaurant owners: write from the email address you registered with, and include your restaurant name.
  3. Customers: include the name and phone number you used when placing orders, booking tables or leaving feedback, so we can find your records.
  4. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Restaurant owner accounts:

  • Your login account (email address and password)
  • Your restaurant profile, logo and menu images
  • Your menu, order records, table bookings and feedback stored for your restaurant
  • Location details provided during registration

Customers (no account is needed to use the app):

  • Your name, phone number and delivery address stored with your orders, bookings and feedback
  • The order, booking and feedback records linked to the details you send us

What may be kept

We may keep information we are required to keep by law, or that is needed to prevent fraud or abuse. If we keep anything, we will tell you in our reply. Backup copies are removed within 30 days after deletion.

Questions

Contact minahilamir2012@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
