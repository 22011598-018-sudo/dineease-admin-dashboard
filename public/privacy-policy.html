DineEase Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

DineEase (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a restaurant ordering and table booking app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using DineEase you agree to this policy.

1. Who uses the app

  • Customers browse restaurants, place food orders, book tables and leave feedback. No account is required to do this.
  • Restaurant owners register a restaurant account to manage their menu, orders, table bookings and feedback.

2. Information we collect

From customers:

  • Name and phone number, entered when placing an order, booking a table or leaving feedback.
  • Delivery address and order notes, if you provide them.
  • Order details (items, quantities, totals, payment method selected, status and time).
  • Table booking details (date, time, number of guests, purpose).
  • Feedback you submit (rating and comment).

From restaurant owners:

  • Email address and login credentials, used to sign in.
  • Restaurant details such as name, logo and menu images, and business information entered during registration.
  • Location: during registration, the app may ask for your device location (approximate or precise) so that our administrator can verify the restaurant’s address. Location is requested only while you are using the app for this purpose. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Photos you choose to upload (for example a logo or menu photos), using the camera or your photo library, only when you ask the app to do so.

The app does not contain advertising and does not sell your personal information.

3. How we use information

  • To send your order, booking or feedback to the restaurant you selected.
  • To let restaurants contact you about your order or booking (for example by phone).
  • To let restaurant owners manage their menu, orders and bookings, and to let our administrator verify and manage restaurant accounts.
  • To keep the app working, secure and free of abuse.

4. Who can see your information

  • The restaurant you order from or book with can see the details you submit to it.
  • Our administrator can view restaurant account information and notifications.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

5. Service providers

DineEase uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database and Storage) to store data and sign restaurant owners in. Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google’s own privacy and security terms. Data is sent over secure connections.

6. Permissions the app may request

  • Location: restaurant registration and verification only.
  • Camera and photos: to upload restaurant images, only when you choose to.
  • Internet access: required to use the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep order, booking and feedback records for as long as needed to operate the service and for the restaurant’s records. You can ask us to delete or correct your data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Restaurant owners can also ask for their account and related data to be deleted. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

DineEase is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date shown at the top.
